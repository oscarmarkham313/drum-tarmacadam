Exteriors that look maintained

In an Irish climate, exterior surfaces grow a film of algae, moss and dirt within a couple of seasons. It happens slowly enough that most people stop noticing — until the surface is cleaned and the difference is stark. Beyond appearance, moss-covered paving and steps get genuinely slippery in wet weather, which matters a great deal on commercial premises where the public walk.

Commercial forecourts and premises

For businesses, the entrance is the first thing a customer sees. Forecourts, footpaths, loading areas, bin stores and building facades all benefit, and work can be scheduled outside trading hours to avoid disruption.

Domestic driveways and patios

Driveways, patios, garden paths and decking are cleaned back to the original surface. Where appropriate, cleaning is a sensible precursor to resealing or resurfacing — and often the cheaper answer where a surface is dirty rather than damaged.

Frequently asked questions

Will pressure washing damage my paving?

Not when the right pressure and technique are used for the surface. Different materials need different approaches, which we assess on site.

How often should a driveway be cleaned?

Most Irish driveways benefit from cleaning every one to two years, depending on shade, tree cover and drainage.

Do you work outside business hours?

Yes. Commercial cleaning is frequently scheduled for evenings or weekends so trading is unaffected.

Do you cover both commercial and domestic?

Yes — everything from a single domestic driveway to ongoing commercial premises cleaning across County Limerick.
