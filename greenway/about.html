Our Story

Your Property.
Our Priority.

Greenway Property Maintenance & Facilities Management was founded with one simple goal: to take the stress out of property maintenance for landlords, businesses and homeowners across Limerick and surrounding areas.

Based at The Old Creamery Enterprise Centre in Ardagh, Co. Limerick, we've grown from a small local team into a trusted regional business — serving clients from Limerick to Tipperary and beyond.

Our approach is simple: one call, every trade, every job. We coordinate experienced tradespeople, manage timelines and communicate clearly — so you don't have to chase anyone. From a leaking tap to a full office refurbishment, we handle it all.

Fully insured and all work carried out to the highest professional standards.

Trusted network of qualified tradespeople across all disciplines.

Transparent pricing — no hidden costs, no surprises.
