Preparation is most of the job

Anyone can apply paint. The difference between a repaint that still looks right in five years and one that flakes within two is almost entirely in what happened before the first coat: filling, caulking, sanding, stabilising powdery surfaces and treating any damp or rot rather than sealing it in. Preparation is where the time goes, and it is not the part to shortcut.

Exterior work in an Irish climate

Exterior painting here has to cope with persistent damp, wind-driven rain and very short reliable weather windows. That affects product choice and timing as much as technique — particularly on fascias, soffits, render and exposed elevations that take the brunt of the weather.

Commercial repaints

Offices, retail units and commercial premises can be repainted in phases or outside trading hours so the business keeps running. Full colour changes, refresh coats and post-refurbishment decoration are all routine.

What this includes

  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings, woodwork
  • Exterior painting and render coating
  • Fascia, soffit and gutter painting
  • Wallpaper hanging and removal
  • Full surface preparation and repairs
  • Commercial and office repaints

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover interior and exterior?

Yes, both — from a single room to a full commercial repaint, inside and out.

Do you prepare surfaces or just paint?

Preparation is included as standard: filling, caulking, sanding and repairing damaged plaster before any paint goes on.

Can you work outside business hours?

Yes. Commercial decorating is often scheduled for evenings or weekends to avoid disrupting trade.

Do you supply the paint?

We can supply everything, or work with paint you have specified — whichever suits.
