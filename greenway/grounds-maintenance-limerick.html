Grounds that reflect the business

Unkempt grounds undermine an otherwise well-run premises. Overgrown grass, straggling hedges and weeds through the paving read as neglect long before anyone gets inside the building. Regular maintenance is a small recurring cost that protects a much larger impression.

Contract maintenance

For business parks, estates and commercial properties, scheduled visits keep everything consistently presentable rather than lurching between overgrown and freshly cut. Frequency is set to suit the site and the season — typically more frequent through the growing months, tapering into autumn and winter.

One-off clearances

Where grounds have got away from a property — a vacant house, an inherited property, a site being prepared for sale or letting — a one-off clearance brings it back under control, and a maintenance schedule keeps it that way if wanted.

What this includes

  • Grass cutting and lawn maintenance
  • Hedge trimming and shaping
  • Weed control
  • Seasonal clean-ups
  • Site and garden clearance
  • Ongoing grounds maintenance contracts
