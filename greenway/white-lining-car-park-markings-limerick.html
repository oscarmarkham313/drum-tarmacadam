Markings that organise a site

Good line marking is quietly doing a lot of work: it maximises the number of usable spaces, keeps traffic flowing in one direction, keeps pedestrians away from reversing vehicles, and makes accessible bays properly usable. A faded car park does the opposite — spaces get wasted, cars park awkwardly, and the site looks neglected before anyone reaches the door.

Refresh and re-mark

Markings fade with traffic, weather and cleaning. Re-marking an existing layout is quick and inexpensive, and is one of the highest-impact things a commercial premises can do for appearance. Where a layout no longer suits the site, we can set out a new one to make better use of the available space.

Working around your business

Most commercial car parks cannot simply close. Work is generally programmed in sections, or scheduled for evenings and weekends, so the premises keeps operating while marking proceeds and paint cures.

What this includes

  • Car park bay markings
  • Disabled and accessible bay markings
  • Directional arrows and hatching
  • No-parking and loading zones
  • Road and access markings
  • Line re-marking and refresh

Frequently asked questions

How long before cars can drive on new lines?

Curing time depends on the paint system and weather, but sections are typically usable the same day. We confirm timings on site so you can plan access.

Can you mark out a new car park layout?

Yes. We can set out a fresh layout to make better use of the space, including bay sizes, circulation routes and accessible bays.

Do you mark private roads and yards?

Yes — access roads, industrial yards, loading areas and internal site roads are all routine work.

Do you cover schools and public areas?

Yes, throughout County Limerick, and work can be scheduled outside operating hours where required.
