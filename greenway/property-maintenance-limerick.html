Maintenance that keeps properties working

Buildings do not fail on a schedule. A blocked gully in October, a failed socket the week a tenant moves in, a car park that has quietly worn through to the base course — the work is constant and it rarely arrives one job at a time. That is the problem Greenway is built around. Rather than sourcing a different contractor for every trade, you get one number, one point of contact and one team coordinating the lot.

Planned and reactive cover

Planned preventative maintenance keeps small defects from becoming expensive ones: seasonal gutter and gully clearing, exterior repainting before weather gets in, line markings refreshed before they fade to nothing. Reactive maintenance handles what you cannot plan for, with emergency call-outs available 24/7 for genuine emergencies such as escaping water, storm damage or anything that makes a property unsafe or unlettable.

Who we work with

Landlords and letting agents managing rental stock, business parks and commercial landlords, industrial units, retail premises, and homeowners who would rather make one call than chase five tradespeople. Work ranges from a single afternoon repair to a full refurbishment managed start to finish.

What this includes

  • General repairs and handyman services
  • Planned preventative maintenance
  • 24/7 emergency call-outs
  • Structural repairs
  • Door and window repairs
  • End-of-tenancy make-good
