If you're considering updating the look of your windows, you have two options: replace them entirely, or have them professionally sprayed. Most homeowners who get quotes for both are surprised by the difference. Here's an honest comparison from Spray Craft — the specialists who spray windows professionally every day across Dublin and Ireland.

Cost of Window Replacement in Ireland

  • A standard casement window replacement: €400–€800 per window, fitted
  • A full house of windows (10–15 windows): €5,000–€12,000+
  • Installation involves removing existing frames, potential redecoration, and days of disruption
  • Lead time: typically 4–8 weeks from order to installation

Cost of Professional Window Spraying

  • Professional window spraying is significantly cheaper than replacement
  • A full house of windows is typically completed in a single day
  • No removal, no disruption, no redecoration required
  • Result: a factory-quality colour finish with a written 5-year guarantee
  • Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your property

Is Spraying as Good as New Windows?

  • In terms of appearance: yes — a professionally sprayed window is indistinguishable from a factory-coloured one
  • In terms of performance: if your existing windows seal and operate correctly, spraying doesn't change that
  • If your windows have seal failure, condensation between panes, or structural issues, replacement is the right answer — but if they're just the wrong colour, spraying is the obvious choice

When to Choose Spraying Over Replacement

  • Your windows function correctly but are the wrong colour
  • You want to update your home's exterior without a major project
  • You're preparing to sell and want to maximise kerb appeal
  • You want the job done in a day, not weeks
