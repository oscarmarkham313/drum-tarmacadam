Your front door is the first thing visitors see. If it's faded, chipped, or simply the wrong colour, door spraying is the fastest and most cost-effective way to transform your home's kerb appeal. Spray Craft's team covers all of Dublin — and the results speak for themselves.

We spray doors on-site, in place. That means no removal costs, no waiting weeks for a new door, and no disruption to your home. Most front door jobs are completed within a single day, with the door ready to use again by evening.

Door Types We Spray

  • Composite doors (GRP and fibreglass faced)
  • uPVC front and back doors
  • Timber and hardwood doors
  • Aluminium entrance doors
  • Patio doors and sliding doors
  • Garage doors (up-and-over and sectional)

Popular Door Colours in Dublin

Anthracite Grey (RAL 7016) is the most in-demand colour we spray — a bold, modern finish that lifts even older homes. Other popular choices include:

  • Jet Black (RAL 9005)
  • Irish Green / Heritage Green
  • Navy Blue (RAL 5011)
  • Traffic Red (RAL 3020)
  • Cream White (RAL 9001)
  • Any colour — we match any RAL or BS reference

Our Door Spraying Process

  • Free site visit and quote — we assess the door and advise on prep requirements
  • Thorough cleaning, degreasing, and sanding to ensure adhesion
  • Glass, hardware, and surrounding areas fully masked
  • Professional spray application — multiple coats of 2K two-component paint
  • Final inspection and sign-off with you on site
  • Written 5-year paint guarantee issued on completion

Cost of Door Spraying in Dublin

Door spraying typically costs a fraction of a replacement door — especially for composite or timber doors where a like-for-like replacement can run into thousands. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your door type and colour choice.
