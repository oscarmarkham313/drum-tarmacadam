If you have white uPVC windows across your Dublin home, uPVC window spraying in Dublin is the fastest and most cost-effective way to transform them. Spray Craft covers all Dublin postcodes, bringing a full professional spray setup to your property.

Unlike cheaper single-coat brush-on systems, we use a two-component (2K) spray-applied paint that chemically hardens after application — the same technology used in commercial and automotive finishing. The result is factory-grade colour that's built to last a decade or more.

What We Cover Across Dublin

Our Process

  • Professional 2K spray application — multiple coats
  • Inspection and sign-off — written 5-year guarantee issued on completion
