White uPVC windows are everywhere in Ireland — but they're not the only option. With professional uPVC window spraying, Spray Craft can change the colour of your existing uPVC frames to any shade you choose, leaving you with a fresh, modern look that lasts.

Our 2K two-component paint system bonds directly to uPVC, producing a hard-wearing finish that resists chipping, fading, and weathering. Unlike cheaper single-component paints, our system is the same grade used by commercial applicators across Europe — and every job comes with a written 5-year guarantee.

Can You Really Spray uPVC?

Yes — and when done correctly, it's permanent. The key is proper surface preparation: thorough cleaning, degreasing, and light sanding to create a key for the paint, followed by a bonding primer and multiple coats of 2K topcoat. Done right, sprayed uPVC is indistinguishable from a factory-coloured frame.

We do not use brush-on paint, roller systems, or single-component paints — only professional spray-applied 2K systems that meet commercial standards.

How Long Does It Last?

With our 2K paint system and proper prep, the finish should last 10–15 years under normal conditions. We back every job with a written 5-year paint guarantee — if the paint fades, peels, or chips under normal use within 5 years, we'll return and fix it at no cost to you.
