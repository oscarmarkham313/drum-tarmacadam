Composite doors are a major investment — and their colour is one of the first things people notice about your home. If your composite door is faded, sun-bleached, or simply the wrong colour for your updated exterior, composite door spraying is the most cost-effective solution.

Spray Craft specialises in spraying GRP (glass-reinforced plastic) and fibreglass composite doors to any colour. The key to a lasting result on composite is correct surface preparation — the GRP skin requires specific priming before topcoat application. Our team is trained and experienced in composite-specific finishes.

Why Spray Your Composite Door?

  • A new composite door can cost €1,500–€3,000+ installed — spraying costs a fraction of that
  • The door structure, hardware, and seals are untouched — only the colour changes
  • Our 2K paint system is specifically formulated to bond to GRP and fibreglass
  • Factory-quality finish that's indistinguishable from a new door in the colour you choose
  • Written 5-year guarantee on every job

Composite Door Types We Spray

  • GRP (glass-reinforced plastic) composite doors — all brands
  • Fibreglass composite front doors
  • Part-glazed and fully-panelled composite doors
  • Composite back and side doors
  • Door frames and surrounds (uPVC or timber)

Popular Composite Door Colours

The most common request we get is Anthracite Grey (RAL 7016) — it gives composite doors a sleek, contemporary look that's hugely popular across Ireland right now. Other regularly requested colours:

  • Jet Black (RAL 9005)
  • Chartwell Green / Irish Green
  • Duck Egg Blue
  • Racing Green (RAL 6005)
  • Traffic Red (RAL 3020)
  • Any RAL or BS colour

Our Process for Composite Doors

  • Free on-site assessment and quote
  • Door thoroughly cleaned and degreased
  • GRP/fibreglass surface lightly abraded to create mechanical key
  • Specialist primer coat applied to ensure adhesion to composite
  • Multiple coats of 2K topcoat applied by spray
  • Glass panels, hardware, letterbox, and frame fully masked throughout
  • Inspection and sign-off — written guarantee issued
