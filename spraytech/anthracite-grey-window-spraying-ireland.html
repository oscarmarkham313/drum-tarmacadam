Anthracite Grey (RAL 7016) is the most requested window colour in Ireland. It gives any home — from red-brick Dublin terraces to modern detached builds — a sharp, contemporary edge that white uPVC simply can't match. Spray Craft applies RAL 7016 across Ireland to all window frame materials.

We spray anthracite grey to uPVC, aluminium, and timber window frames using a professional 2K two-component paint system. The finish is hard-wearing, UV-resistant, and far more durable than DIY painting solutions. Every job comes with a written 5-year paint guarantee.
