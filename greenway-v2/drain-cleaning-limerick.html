Clearing blockages properly

A blocked drain is usually a symptom rather than the whole story. Clearing the immediate blockage gets the property working again, but if the same gully blocks every autumn, or a section of pipe has collapsed or bellied, the problem returns. Where a blockage keeps recurring, a CCTV survey shows exactly what is happening underground so the repair addresses the cause.

Storm water and surface water

Ireland does not lack rainfall, and surface water is one of the most common causes of avoidable property damage. Blocked gullies, silted channels, inadequate falls and undersized outfalls all end the same way — water going where it should not. We clear and maintain gullies and channels, and where flooding is a recurring risk, we implement drainage solutions that manage the water rather than moving the problem a few metres.

Planned gully maintenance

For commercial sites, scheduled gully and channel cleaning ahead of autumn and winter is far cheaper than an emergency call-out and a flooded unit. Maintenance contracts can be arranged to suit the site.

Frequently asked questions

Do you attend emergency blockages?

Yes. Emergency drainage call-outs are available 24/7 — call 083 030 1799 for anything urgent such as flooding or sewage backing up.

What causes drains to keep blocking?

Recurring blockages usually indicate an underlying defect: root ingress, a collapsed or bellied section, silt build-up or a fall that is too shallow. A CCTV survey identifies which.

Do you clean commercial gullies on contract?

Yes. Scheduled gully and channel cleaning can be arranged for business parks, industrial units and commercial premises.

Do you cover rural properties?

Yes, throughout County Limerick including rural areas around Ardagh, Newcastle West, Rathkeale and Abbeyfeale.
