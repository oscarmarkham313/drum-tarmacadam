Carpentry & joinery

Doors, floors, built-in storage and second-fix work for homes and commercial property across Limerick and Kerry.

Carpentry is unforgiving in a way other trades are not

A door that binds. A floor that creaks in the same spot. A fitted unit a few millimetres out of square. These are the details people notice every single day, long after they have forgotten what the walls cost to paint.

Older Irish properties are rarely square

Which is exactly why off-the-shelf units so often sit badly in them. Built-in work made to the actual opening solves problems that flat-pack cannot.

Second fix is where sequencing shows

Carpentry is usually one element of a bigger job. Done in the wrong order - before plastering has dried, after decoration has finished - it costs twice.

Signs you need this

  • Doors sticking, dropping or no longer latching
  • Floors creaking, lifting or springing underfoot
  • Gaps opening at skirting, architrave or stair strings
  • Storage that does not fit the space it is in
  • A refurbishment where trades keep arriving in the wrong order

What we cover

  • Door hanging and replacement
  • Timber and laminate flooring
  • Built-in storage and wardrobes
  • Skirting, architrave and second fix
  • Stud partitions and framing
  • General building and joinery works

How the job runs

In order, and in writing.

Measure the actual opening

Not the nominal size. This is the whole difference in older buildings.

Confirm the sequence

Where the carpentry sits relative to plastering, electrical and decoration.

Prepare the substrate

Levels and fixings checked before anything is fitted.

Fit

Hung, levelled, scribed and packed as the opening actually requires.

Finish

Trim, adjust and leave it operating correctly, not just installed.

When you don't need us

Hanging a single internal door in a modern, square opening is a genuinely achievable DIY job, and we will not pretend otherwise. Bring us in for made-to-measure work, older properties where nothing is true, structural carpentry, or anything forming part of a multi-trade job.
