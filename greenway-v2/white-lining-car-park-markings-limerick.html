White lining & car parks

Line marking for car parks, business premises, schools and public areas across Limerick and Kerry.

Markings are doing more work than they look

Good layout maximises usable spaces, keeps traffic moving one way, keeps pedestrians away from reversing vehicles and makes accessible bays genuinely usable. A faded car park does the opposite - spaces get wasted, cars park at angles, and the site looks neglected before anyone reaches reception.

Re-marking is the cheapest improvement a premises can make

Markings fade with traffic, weather and cleaning. Refreshing an existing layout is quick and inexpensive, and few things lift the look of a commercial property faster for the money.

Layout can be changed, not just repainted

If the current arrangement no longer suits how the site is used, there is usually room to set out a better one - more spaces, safer circulation, or proper delivery access.

Signs you need this

  • Bays are no longer clearly visible in the wet or at night
  • Drivers are parking across lines or at inconsistent angles
  • Accessible bays lack proper markings or hatched transfer space
  • You have resurfaced and the markings have not been reinstated
  • Traffic flow around the site causes near-misses

What we cover

  • Car park bay markings
  • Accessible and disabled bay markings
  • Directional arrows and hatching
  • No-parking and loading zones
  • Road and access markings
  • Line re-marking and refresh
  • Numbering and reserved bays

Before you get quotes

What determines the price.

We do not publish figures, because a figure without a site visit is a guess. These are the variables that actually move the number - worth understanding whoever you end up using.

Number and type of markings

Straight bays are quick. Arrows, hatching, lettering and disabled symbols take longer.

New layout versus refresh

Re-marking existing lines is faster than setting out a new arrangement from scratch.

Surface condition

Paint on a failing or contaminated surface will not hold, so preparation can be a real part of the cost.

Paint system

Different products suit different traffic loads and expected life.

Access and timing

Out-of-hours or phased working to keep a site trading carries cost, but usually far less than closing.

When you don't need us

If your markings are simply dirty rather than worn, a clean may bring them back - worth checking before repainting. And if the surface underneath is breaking up, marking it now is money wasted; the surfacing should come first.

Questions

Straight answers.

How long before cars can use the bays?

Curing depends on the paint system and the weather, but sections are typically usable the same day. We confirm timings on site so you can plan access.

Can you design a new layout?

Yes - bay sizes, circulation, accessible bays and delivery access can all be set out fresh to make better use of the space.

Do you mark private roads and yards?

Yes, including industrial yards, loading areas and internal site roads.

Can you work outside opening hours?

Yes, and for schools and retail premises that is usually how it is scheduled.

Do you cover Kerry?

Yes, Limerick and Kerry.
